Gizlilik Politikası

Support (English)

Deutschef is a German-Turkish kitchen language app for cooks who want to work in Germany.

For questions, feedback or issues, email us: BURAYA_EPOSTA

How do I cancel my subscription? On your iPhone, go to Settings > your Apple ID > Subscriptions, select Deutschef Pro and cancel.

How do I delete my account? Use the account deletion option in the app's settings screen.
